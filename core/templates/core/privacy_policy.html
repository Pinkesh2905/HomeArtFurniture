Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This system was built and is maintained by Pinkesh ("the developer") for Home Art Furniture ("the business"). This page explains, for the business owner and staff using this system, what data is stored, who can access it, and how the developer handles it. It is not addressed to the business's furniture customers, who never log in to or interact directly with this system.

What data this system stores

  • Customer records: name, phone number, city, and order/measurement history
  • Order and billing details: items, prices, discounts, and payment records
  • Inventory and supplier records
  • Staff login accounts (username and password) used to access the system

This is your business's data — you own it. The developer stores it on your behalf, purely so the software can function.

Who can access it

  • Your own staff, via the individual logins you create for them
  • The developer, who — as the person hosting and maintaining the system — technically has the ability to access the underlying database when needed for support, debugging, or maintenance

The developer will not view, use, sell, or share your business or customer data for any purpose other than building, fixing, or maintaining this system for you. Access for support is only ever to help you, never to repurpose your data.

Where it's hosted

The database runs on Supabase (PostgreSQL hosting), and the application itself runs on Render. Both are third-party infrastructure providers — they host the servers but have no independent access to or use of your business data beyond that.

Security

The system requires a login for every staff user, encrypts traffic (HTTPS) in production, and follows reasonable security practices for a system of this size. That said, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure — please keep staff passwords private and report anything that looks wrong immediately.

If you stop using this system

Your data remains yours. If you ever want to stop using this system, you can request a full export of your business data, and/or its deletion from the hosting infrastructure, at any time.
